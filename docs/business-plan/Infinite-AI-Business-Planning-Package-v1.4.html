1Executive summary

Infinite-AI is a school operating system, not a chatbot. The two repositories audited here describe a platform where AI drafts curriculum, support and professional-development material, and a teacher approves it ("AI drafts. The teacher decides. Affordability check

The 2026 DBE per-learner allocation is R1,835 for quintile 1 to 3, R919 for quintile 4 and R315 for quintile 5 schools (gazette figures as cited in the project notes via FEDSA/PMG; confirm against the gazette before quoting to a school). VAT treatment

  • Public schools are generally not VAT vendors, so the VAT-inclusive price is what their budget absorbs. Prices are quoted VAT-inclusive with the ex-VAT split shown on every invoice (D-2).
  • Decision D-6: Infinite-AI registers for VAT voluntarily from launch, subject to your accountant, so that input VAT on AWS, tools and services can be reclaimed (the compulsory threshold is R2.3 million of taxable turnover in 12 months, applicable from 1 April 2026). Confirm the route and timing with your accountant.
  • In 2027 and 2028 input VAT exceeds output VAT, giving net VAT refunds of R0.12m and R0.12m (Base). From 2029 the business becomes a net VAT payer (R0.19m in 2029, R1.24m in 2031).
  • Imported cloud and model services (AWS, Anthropic) may carry reverse-charge VAT rather than invoice VAT; the model treats VAT on them as claimable input VAT at 15%. Confirm with an adviser.

What teacher training is, and is not

  • Decision D-3: teacher training is included in the ecosystem. It is not a second product line and the model books no training revenue. 17How to use and edit this package

  • HTML file: open it in any browser. Press the "Edit mode" button at the top right, click on any text, change it, then press "Save copy" to download your edited page. Charts are inline vector graphics and stay sharp when printed or exported to PDF (use the browser's Print > Save as PDF).
  • Word file: open in Word, edit as normal. Tables are real tables, so figures can be corrected in place. Use References > Update table to refresh the contents page after edits.
  • Numbers: they come from one Python model. To change an assumption (for example the Starter price), ask Claude to re-run it with the new value and regenerate both files, so that every table, chart and VAT column stays consistent.
  • AI Studio: send an export or screenshots and the audit section will be extended.
  • Before sharing outside the school: have an accountant review sections 5, 7 and 11, and an attorney review section 9.
