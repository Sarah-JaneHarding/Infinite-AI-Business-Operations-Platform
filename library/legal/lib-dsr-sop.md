## Steps

1. Log the request on the day it arrives (lib-dsr-form).
2. Confirm the requester’s identity.
3. Decide who is responsible: the school or the company. If the school, pass it on the same day.
4. Find the data. Use the platform’s purpose-checked access, not a direct database query.
5. Check for a legal reason to refuse or limit. Counsel decides.
6. Respond in writing within [Fill in: response time set by counsel].
7. Record the outcome. A deletion goes through the retention and tombstone path, never a manual delete.

## Do not

- Delete anything by hand in the data store.
- Send personal information by unprotected email.
