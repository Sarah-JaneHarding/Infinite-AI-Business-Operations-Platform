### 1. Definitions

Define: Platform, Services, Order Form, Educator, Learner, School Data, Personal Information, De-identified Data, Term, Fees, Business Day.

### 2. Services and licence

State what the subscription includes by reference to the Order Form, and grant a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the platform for the school’s own educational purposes.

### 3. School responsibilities

Lawful basis for the data the school loads, authorised users only, credentials kept safe, no unlawful content.

### 4. Fees and payment

Fees follow the Order Form. State the invoice date, the payment term and what happens on late payment.

[Fill in: payment term, late-payment position, whether prices are quoted VAT-inclusive; decide with counsel and the tax adviser]

### 5. Personal information

The school is the responsible party and the supplier is the operator. Incorporate the Data Processing Agreement (lib-dpa) as a schedule. Say that learner personal information is never placed in a prompt to a model and is de-identified first, matching the product’s privacy design.

### 6. Intellectual property

Supplier keeps the platform. School keeps School Data. State ownership of content the school creates with the tools.

[Fill in: licence terms for school-created content]

### 7. Confidentiality

### 8. Service levels

Refer to the Service Level Agreement (lib-sla) as a schedule.

### 9. Warranties and AI output
