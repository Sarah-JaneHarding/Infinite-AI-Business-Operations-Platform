## Rules built into the platform

- Every model call goes through the Model Gateway. There is no second route.
- No learner personal information enters a prompt. Data is de-identified and stamped first.
- Anything that affects a learner needs a human approval record before it takes effect.
- AI output is a draft, never a decision.
- Facts in the Brain are superseded with a new version, not overwritten.

## Rules for staff

- Do not paste learner information into any outside AI tool.
- Do not accept AI output without reading it.
- Report mistakes and unsafe output.
