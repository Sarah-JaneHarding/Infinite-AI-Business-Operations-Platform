## First hour

1. Stop the harm: revoke access, rotate affected secrets, isolate the system.
2. Tell the incident lead ([Fill in: role]).
3. Start a log. Write down times and facts, not guesses.
4. Preserve evidence; do not wipe systems.

## Assess

- What data? Whose? How many people?
- Was learner information involved?
- Is the data readable by the person who got it?
- Is it still happening?

## Notify

Where the law requires it, the responsible party notifies the Regulator and the affected people. As operator the company tells the school without undue delay. Counsel decides what is notified and when.
