## Principles

- Each school’s data is isolated in the database, enforced by the database.
- No learner personal information in any AI prompt.
- Least access: roles get only what they need.
- Every access to learner data has a declared purpose and a lawful basis.
- Audit and consent records are append-only.
- No secret in code or documents.
