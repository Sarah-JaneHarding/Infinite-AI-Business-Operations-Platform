Tagline: EDUCATE - INNOVATE - TRANSFORM
Contact: 10 Jellicoe Street, Pinetown, KwaZulu-Natal, 3610, South Africa | +27 611 939 620 | infinite-ai-ecosystem@protonmail.com

Start with `Infinite-AI-Brand-Guidelines.html` (or the PDF). It lists every file, its size and what it is for.

- `social/` LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, X, Instagram, WhatsApp, founder card, pull-up banner
- `web/` website hero, email header, video-call background, link-preview image
- `slides/` presentation backgrounds
- `icons/` logo master, favicons, founder photo sizes
- `print/` letterhead, compliments slip, document cover, business cards (3 mm bleed)
- `docs/` editable Word letterhead
- `email/` signatures and install steps
- `brand-tokens.json` colours, fonts, contact details

Limits to know: the founder photo is 360 x 360 px, so it is used at that size or smaller; a larger original is needed for big prints. The letterhead Word file has two blanks for the company registration and VAT numbers, which were not supplied.
