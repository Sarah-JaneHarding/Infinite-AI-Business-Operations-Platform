# Install your email signature

Your business email is infinite-ai-ecosystem@protonmail.com (a Proton Mail address).

## Proton Mail
1. Open `email-signature-founder.html` in Chrome or Edge (keep the `images` folder beside it).
2. Press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac) to select everything, then Ctrl+C to copy.
3. In Proton Mail: Settings > All settings > Identity and addresses > Edit next to your address > Signature.
4. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V). If the photo does not paste, use the image button in the signature toolbar and upload `images/founder-96.png` and `images/logo-120.png`.
5. Save. Send yourself a test email and check it on your phone.

The menu names can change slightly between versions. If you cannot find it, search the Proton Mail help for "signature".

## Gmail or Outlook
Use `email-signature-plain-text.txt` if the pasted images do not appear. Images in a signature usually need to be uploaded through the signature editor's image button, using the two PNG files in `images/`.

## Team members
Use `email-signature-team-template.html` and replace the two `[Fill in: ...]` parts with the person's name and title. Never use a learner's photo or details in a signature.
